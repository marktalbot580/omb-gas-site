The Tools

Four calculators. No second app.

Everything you would normally switch apps for, built in, and you can show your workings.

Gas rate

Time the meter in metric or imperial. Start the timer before you have any readings, then get the gas rate and the gross and net heat input to check against the data plate.

Gas pipe sizing

Worked out from the published BS 6891 copper pipe capacity table. It also shows how many metres of the bigger pipe you need nearest the meter before the run can drop a size.

Heat loss and radiators

Go room by room. Radiator sizes change with your flow and return temperatures, so the numbers match the system you are fitting.

Works out the installation volume and the pressure drop allowed under the IGEM/UP/1B Edition 4 rules that start on 1 October 2026.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need a signal?

No. The forms, calculators and invoices all work offline. Your work syncs to your account when you are back online.

What phone do I need?

Any iPhone or Android phone with a normal browser. Open the app, choose “Add to Home Screen”, and it opens full screen like any other app.

Can the app remind my customers when their check is due?

Yes. Turn on automatic emails in Settings and customers with an email address are emailed about 8 weeks and 2 weeks before. You can switch it off for any customer, and anyone can unsubscribe from the email. The Annual checks due list shows who has been sent a reminder, and gives you ready-written texts and WhatsApps for the rest.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone first, and backed up to your account whenever you are online. You can also save a backup file yourself at any time.

Is it approved by the Gas Safe Register?

No. OMB Gas Service is independent and is not affiliated with the Gas Safe Register. You are still responsible for the checks you carry out and for what you sign.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel any time from Settings. If your subscription ends, you can still view, download and export your records.

Something is not working, or I have an idea.

Use Send feedback in the app’s Settings, or email support@ombgas.com.
